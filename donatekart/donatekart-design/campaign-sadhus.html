About This Campaign

Elderly Sadhus have renounced worldly possessions to pursue spiritual enlightenment. Many lack basic necessities like nutritious food and healthcare. Our campaign ensures they receive daily meals and essential medical support.

What Your Donation Supports

  • Daily nutritious meals for elderly Sadhus
  • Healthcare and medical check-ups
  • Warm clothing and basic necessities
  • Shelter and accommodation support
  • Spiritual guidance and mental wellness programs

How Funds Are Used

Daily Food & Nutrition (50%)
Healthcare (25%)
Clothing & Supplies (15%)
Admin & Operations (10%)
