About This Campaign

Millions of people in India struggle with hunger daily. Our campaign provides hot, nutritious meals to those in need, from homeless individuals to migrant workers to underfed children. Every meal is an act of compassion.

What Your Donation Supports

  • Hot, nutritious meals for hungry individuals
  • Meals for homeless and street children
  • Mid-day meals in schools in underprivileged areas
  • Food packages for struggling families
  • Nutrition and hygiene awareness programs

How Funds Are Used

Direct Meals & Food (55%)
School Meal Programs (20%)
Transportation & Distribution (15%)
Admin & Operations (10%)
