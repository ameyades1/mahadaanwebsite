About This Campaign

Thousands of elderly Indians are abandoned by their families, left to fend for themselves on the streets. Our campaign provides them with safe shelter, nutritious food, healthcare, and the dignity and respect they deserve.

What Your Donation Supports

  • Safe, clean shelter in elder care homes
  • Three nutritious meals daily
  • Comprehensive healthcare and medications
  • Emotional support and counseling
  • Activities and recreation programs

How Funds Are Used

Shelter & Accommodation (40%)
Food & Nutrition (30%)
Healthcare (20%)
Admin & Operations (10%)
