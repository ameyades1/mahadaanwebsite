# Mahadaan Website Wireframe Design Prompt for Google Stitch

Design wireframes for the Antar Yog Foundation Mahadaan donation website. The website should facilitate charitable giving across 17 distinct donation categories with a clean, organized information architecture.

## Core Pages & Structure

### 1. Main Homepage

- Hero section with headline and sub-headline
- 17 donation category cards arranged in a grid (Anna Daan, Special Occasions, Mahadaan, Sadhu/Sanyasi Seva, Brahman Seva, Bal Seva, Orphan Seva, Divyang Seva, Suvasini Seva, Serve The Needy, Elder Care, Aarogya Seva, Guru Daan, Gau Seva, Books Daan, Gurukul Daan, Daan Through Seva)
- Section explaining why Antar Yog Daan is unique (4-5 key points: Amplified, Rooted in Sanatan Dharma, Directed Towards Deserving, Spiritually Powerful, Handled with Responsibility)
- Section for "Why Daan Matters for Doers and Leaders" (bullet point benefits)
- Section "What Your Contribution Supports" (7-8 benefit points)
- "The Vedic Way of Giving" explanatory section (OM TAT SAT Bhav)
- "Supatra Daan" philosophy section
- "Ways To Contribute" section (4 contribution types: One-Time Mahadaan, Monthly Seva Commitment, Annual Patron, Dedicated Daan, Corporate/CSR)
- "Suggested Daan Slabs" table (₹1,100, ₹2,500, ₹5,100, ₹11,000, ₹21,000, ₹51,000, ₹1,00,000+)
- "Legacy and Dedication Options" section
- "Trust and Transparency" section with registration, tax benefits, payment channels
- "Current Seva In Progress" campaign showcase
- "Testimonials" section
- Final CTA section

### 2. Individual Donation Category Pages (Repeated template for each of 17 categories)

Each category page should include:
- Page title and subtitle
- Hero section with descriptive content and CTA button
- "Why This Daan Matters" section with explanation and benefits
- "How Your Contribution Creates Impact" detailed breakdown
- "Suggested Daan/Seva Contributions" table with specific amounts (e.g., "Feed 7 Volunteers - ₹700", "Feed 21 Volunteers - ₹2,100", etc.)
- "Ways To Contribute" options section (one-time, recurring, dedicated, special occasions)
- Tax benefit notice
- "Donor Recognition and Seva Updates" section
- "Trust and Donation Details" with payment methods
- Final CTA button

## Key Information Architecture Principles

- Clear visual hierarchy for all content
- Consistent card-based layout for category listings
- Table layouts for suggested daan amounts
- Multiple CTA buttons throughout each page (emotional messaging in buttons)
- Trust-building elements (registration, tax benefits, payment channels) on every donation page
- Sections clearly delineated with headers
- Contact information for offline contributions
- Tax exemption notices on all pages
- Donor recognition and update promise sections

## Content Elements to Wireframe

- Hero sections with large headlines and supporting copy
- Card components (3-4 columns on desktop)
- Data tables with 2-4 columns (Seva Offering, Amount, Description)
- Testimonial blocks with quotes and attribution
- Feature/benefit list sections with bullet points or icons
- Contact details sections
- CTA buttons with contextual labels
- Form hint text for payment checkout
- Section dividers and spacing

## Tone & Messaging Notes

- Spiritual and emotional language emphasizing gratitude, dharma, and legacy
- Emphasis on impact and the true welfare of recipients
- Reference to scriptural principles (Supatra Daan, OM TAT SAT Bhav)
- Personal connection and dignity for all beneficiaries
- Tax benefits and transparency as trust elements
- Multiple ways to give at different price points
- Dedication and legacy options throughout

Create wireframes that accommodate this content architecture, information density, and multi-tier contribution system while maintaining clarity and user navigation across 17+ distinct donation categories.
